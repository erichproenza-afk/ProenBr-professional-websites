# ProenBR - Professional Websites & Digital Services

## Original Problem Statement
Create a professional bilingual (English/Portuguese) marketing website for ProenBR with:
- Hero Section with headline, subheadline, and CTAs
- About section highlighting experience, fast delivery, professional and affordable services
- Services section (one-page, multi-page, portfolio/landing pages, SEO, custom projects)
- Portfolio with Royal Flush and Deni Cleaning examples
- Testimonials section
- Contact section with USA phone, Brazil WhatsApp, email, Facebook, and contact form
- Language toggle between English and Portuguese

## User Personas
- **Small Business Owners (USA)**: English-speaking clients looking for professional website design
- **Small Business Owners (Brazil)**: Portuguese-speaking clients seeking web development services

## Core Requirements (Static)
1. Bilingual support (EN/PT) with flag toggle
2. WhatsApp CTA integration
3. Smooth scroll navigation
4. Mobile responsive design
5. Professional stock images
6. SEO optimized meta tags
7. Contact form (display only)
